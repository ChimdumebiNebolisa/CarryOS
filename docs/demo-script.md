1. Open `/`. Say: CarryOS helps you know what the day asks of your backpack before you leave.
2. Point at the backpack and the live intelligence overlay. It is a conceptual product view, not validated hardware.
3. Open `/demo`. Generate a carry profile. Show deterministic fallback or model suggestions. Approve nothing yet.
4. Close bag and scan. Notebook is not detected. One warning appears.
5. Open the explanation. Leave by the deterministic travel estimate.
6. Open the bag, add the notebook, scan again.
7. Ready for Algorithms.
8. Disclose: RFID and travel are simulated. The model never decided readiness.
