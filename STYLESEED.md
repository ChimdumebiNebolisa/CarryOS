# STYLESEED

Carry uses a project-local visual lock derived from two approved 21st.dev references and the CarryOS v2 PRD.

- Grammar: `reference:carry-hero`
- Adapter: website / landing + product workspace
- Display: Space Grotesk
- Body: DM Sans
- Utility: DM Mono
- Surfaces: forest/charcoal hero, warm paper content
- Status: forest green confirm, amber uncertainty, red only for critical failure
- Signature: three-layer authentic Carry state stack emerging from the hero, cropped by the viewport
- Motion: state change only; reduced motion shows stable Missing
- Forbidden: orbiting circles, particles, WebGL, testimonials, partner logos, generic AI dashboards

Do not introduce a third visual reference without a recorded material blocker.
